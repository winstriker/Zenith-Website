Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Zenith ("the app") is a pilot's logbook for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. In short: your data stays on your device or in your own cloud storage. We do not run servers, collect analytics, or sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

Flights, aircraft, airports, people, licences, medicals, photos, signatures and settings you enter are stored locally on your device. Credentials (such as cloud access tokens and API keys you provide) are kept in the device's secure storage.

Location

If you start a flight recording, the app uses your device's precise location, also in the background, to record your flight track. Location data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive for backups, the app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This lets Zenith create, read, update and delete only the backup files and folders it created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

  • Google user data is used solely to store and restore your Zenith backups at your request.
  • Data is transferred directly between your device and Google. It never passes through servers we operate.
  • Google user data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI/ML models.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app's backup settings (which revokes the access token) or from your Google Account permissions.

Zenith's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Dropbox

If you connect Dropbox, it is used in the same way: only to store and restore your backups, directly between your device and Dropbox.

Third-party services

To provide some features, the app sends limited, non-personal requests (such as an aircraft registration, ICAO address, callsign or map area) to public services: OpenSky Network, FlyItalyAdsb, adsbdb, JetAPI, Planespotters, OpenStreetMap / CARTO map tiles, OurAirports data, and GitHub (to check for updates). These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

All data remains under your control. Uninstalling the app deletes local data. Backups in Google Drive or Dropbox can be deleted from those services at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
